Probate & Inherited Home Sales in Connecticut
As a Realtor Licensed in Connecticut, I help homeowners and families navigate complex life transitions with clarity, structure, and care. Many of my clients are empty nesters preparing to downsize after decades in one home, as well as heirs inheriting a house with debt and facing difficult, time-sensitive decisions. These situations are rarely just about real estate—they involve finances, family dynamics, and emotional weight.

Whether you’re selling a longtime family home, managing an inherited property from a distance, or repositioning real estate to support your next chapter, my role is to serve as a steady guide through the process. I focus on clear options, realistic timelines, and strategies that protect your interests without adding pressure or confusion.
