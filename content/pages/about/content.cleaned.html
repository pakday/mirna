Meet Realtor Mirna Chinchilla– MC Homes Real Estate

Trusted Guidance for Homeowners Facing Life’s Most Important Real Estate Decisions

Experience, clarity, and calm support for Connecticut sellers navigating probate, downsizing, multi-family sales, and mortgage stress.

For more than two decades, I’ve had the honor of helping Connecticut homeowners through some of the most meaningful, emotional, and financially important moments of their lives. I’m Mirna Chinchilla , owner of MC Homes Real Estate , and my mission is simple: to guide homeowners through challenging, complex, or overwhelming real estate situations with clarity, confidence, and dignity.

My specialty isn’t just selling homes—it’s helping the people behind them. I work with homeowners who need a steady and trustworthy partner, especially when life brings unexpected transitions. Over the years, I’ve become known throughout Danbury and nearby towns for my calm problem-solving, transparent communication, and ability to handle complicated sales that many agents avoid.

I serve homeowners who are downsizing after decades in their home, adult children who suddenly inherit a property and don’t know where to begin, landlords dealing with tenant challenges, and homeowners behind on their mortgage who feel confused or embarrassed about their situation. These aren’t simple transactions; they require sensitivity, steady support, and a clear plan. And that is exactly what I provide.

Your next step starts right here. You’re not stuck—there is a clear, safe path forward. I’ll help you understand your options, avoid costly mistakes, and make confident decisions.

Downsizing & Retiring Sellers

If you're preparing to downsize or retire, you may feel overwhelmed with repairs, belongings, paperwork, or the emotional weight of leaving a longtime home. Your house may need updates, or you may be living part-time in another state. I help downsizing sellers by creating a calm, step-by-step plan, evaluating whether to fix or sell as-is, coordinating repairs, assisting with decluttering, and preparing the home for market. Many of my clients spend winters in Florida and trust me to manage their sale locally while they’re away.

Probate & Inherited Property Sellers

If you're handling a probate or inherited property, the experience can feel overwhelming. There may be belongings left behind, needed repairs, legal steps, or difficult family decisions. I’ve guided many families through this process with patience and compassion. I help coordinate cleanouts, connect you with trusted professionals, recommend repairs only when necessary, and manage the full sale to preserve your family’s equity and reduce stress.

Get the answers you need today. Whether you’re handling a probate home, downsizing after many years, or dealing with repairs or belongings, I’ll help you move forward with clear, simple next steps.

📞 Call 203-744-9879 or visit HomesDanbury.com to start your free, confidential consultation.

Landlords & Investors Selling Multi-Family Homes

If you’re a landlord or investor selling a multi-family home—especially with tenants in place—you need someone who understands tenant communication, access coordination, and investor-level pricing strategies. I’ve helped clients sell properties with difficult tenants, zoning concerns, and health department issues. My job is to keep everything professional, organized, and respectful so you can close smoothly and maximize your return.

Homeowners Facing Mortgage Stress or Pre-Foreclosure

If you're behind on mortgage payments or feeling financial pressure, you may be scared, overwhelmed, or unsure what to do. Many homeowners still have equity and the ability to sell before the bank steps in. I offer confidential, judgment-free conversations to help you understand your timeline and options. Together, we can determine whether selling now will protect your credit and preserve your remaining equity.

Whether you're searching for a condo in Rivington, a townhome near Candlewood Lake, or a home in Bethel, Brookfield, or New Milford, I help you understand neighborhoods, pricing, and long-term value so you can buy with confidence.

Whether I’m helping a family sell their childhood home, guiding a landlord through tenant-occupied showings, or supporting a distressed homeowner through a sensitive financial moment, I approach every situation with care and professionalism.

Over the years, my business has grown through referrals and strong results. Reviews highlight my negotiation skills, responsiveness, and ability to simplify complicated scenarios.
