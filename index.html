My Story

More Than a Realtor —
A Trusted Guide Through Life's Biggest Transitions

I'm Mirna Chinchilla, owner of MC Homes Real Estate. For more than two decades, I've had the honor of guiding Connecticut homeowners through some of the most meaningful, emotional, and financially important moments of their lives.

My specialty isn't just selling homes — it's helping the people behind them. I work with homeowners who need a steady, trustworthy partner, especially when life brings unexpected transitions: downsizing after decades, settling an estate, managing financial stress, or selling from another state.

Over the years, I've become known throughout Danbury and nearby towns for my calm problem-solving, transparent communication, and ability to handle complicated situations that many agents avoid.
